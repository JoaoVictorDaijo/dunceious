Dunceious / environment accent

Design reference · gradient vocabulary for the mode switcher

Make the chrome feel like a place, not a colored edge.

Right now each mode paints one flat gradient strip along the workspace edge. It reads as a status light. The examples below are the same idea done the way Arc, Stripe, and Linear do it — so the sky nucleotide view, the violet protein view, and the amber Database Hub each feel like a room you have walked into. Flip the switch up top and watch every panel change environment together — that is your mode switch, felt.

The gap

What "organic" actually means

A flat strip is one straight line of color, the same everywhere. Organic light does five small things at once — and any one of them already helps.

Now — flat linear stripOne gradient, one direction, hard to love. It sits on top of the UI instead of lighting it.
Organic — layered lightSeveral soft sources, off-center, in one color family, with a whisper of grain. It feels lit.
More than one light source
Off-center, never symmetrical
Hue drifts within one family
A trace of grain, so it reads as material
Motion so slow you feel it, not see it

The toolkit · read top to bottom

Seven ways to build the light

Each technique layers onto the one before it — start with soft light sources, end with barely-there motion. Every panel is live and re-tints when you flip Viewport / Hub.

01

Layered light sources

radial gradients
3 soft radials · off-center

Used by

Vercel's "light itself" hero glows · Linear's launch pages · Raycast wallpapers

Feeling

A room lit from a couple of directions, not a spotlight.

Dunceious mapping

Two or three sky sources bleed in from the viewport corners; swap the family to amber in the Hub.

02

Mesh & blob gradients

stacked radials
4 overlapping blobs · drifting

Used by

Stripe's animated hero · Spotify's per-track adaptive backdrops · most "mesh gradient" tools

Feeling

Ink dropped in water — color dissolving into color, no seams.

Dunceious mapping

A slow sky mesh behind the sequence track; in the Hub the same mesh runs amber-gold.

03

Conic sheen

conic-gradient
angular sweep · softened + vignetted

Used by

Holographic product UI · loading arcs and rings · "iridescent" brand marks

Feeling

Light bending around a point — a faint oil-slick sheen.

Dunceious mapping

A sky halo behind the active molecule so it looks lit from within; rotate it into amber for the Hub.

04

Aurora bands

blurred + screen blend
heavy blur · additive light

Used by

Linear · the whole "Aurora UI" dark-SaaS trend · countless premium heroes

Feeling

Northern lights breathing behind the content.

Dunceious mapping

Two sky bands drift along the top edge of the workspace; the Hub runs warm amber bands instead.

05

Noise & grain

SVG feTurbulence
raw+ grainsame gradient, split down the middle

Used by

Apple product pages · the "grainy gradients" technique · most gradients that don't look cheap

Feeling

Film grain — warm and tactile instead of plasticky. Kills the banding, too.

Dunceious mapping

A ~3% grain over the whole chrome, both modes, so sky and amber read as material, not gloss.

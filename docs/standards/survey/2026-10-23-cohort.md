## Discovery method (W4)

The original 15-app survey and research brief are one seed, not the sole selection basis. The following neutral discovery queries were executed against ELIXIR bio.tools on 2026-10-23, with all pages fetched for the quoted-operation queries. [API documentation](https://biotools.readthedocs.io/en/latest/api_reference.html) says quoted values match an exact phrase. Registry membership establishes discoverability, not adoption.

- [Sequence visualisation](https://bio.tools/api/tool/?format=json&operation=%22Sequence%20visualisation%22): 186 records; all pages inspected as candidate metadata.
- [Sequence editing](https://bio.tools/api/tool/?format=json&operation=%22Sequence%20editing%22): 42 records; all pages inspected as candidate metadata.
- [Sequence annotation](https://bio.tools/api/tool/?format=json&operation=%22Sequence%20annotation%22): 173 records; all pages inspected as candidate metadata.
- [Sequence extraction](https://bio.tools/api/tool/?format=json&operation=%22Sequence%20extraction%22): 0 records; all pages inspected as candidate metadata.

The exact `Sequence extraction` operation returned zero, not evidence of absent extraction tools. Broad `q=sequence extraction` returned365 records and `q=sequence annotation`1415 (first-page diagnostic only; not fully screened). Full-text viewer/editor/browser searches were also diagnostic (80/26/195 hits). The broad feature-extraction search was noisy (478 hits including image and omics feature extraction), so it was not treated as a sequence-tool cohort.

Screening retained general nucleotide/alignment viewers/editors and annotation/extraction engines relevant to LOC-7. Specialized assay dashboards, structure-only viewers, logo plotters, feature predictors, and EMBOSS subcommands already represented by EMBOSS were screened out by scope, not popularity. Candidate metadata identified SeaView, Tablet, GenomeView, SeqMonk, Dalliance, and extraction/annotation utilities beyond the brief. This is a targeted survey, not a systematic exhaustive inventory of every registry result. `registry-screen.json` preserves the fetched records and query ledger.

## Ranking method and caveats

Three groups remain separate: interactive apps; command-line tools/libraries; NCBI/INSDC references. Citation and vendor-user metrics never share a numerical ranking. Paper-selection policy for this bounded candidate audit: freeze the explicitly identified paper set in this table before calculating any capability counts; where multiple tool papers were measured, choose the one with the largest lifetime Europe PMC count, and report other measured papers as sensitivity evidence. This is a reproducible rule within the measured set, NOT a claim to have found every historical tool publication. For Bioconductor, use the framework paper only as a visibly labelled proxy; for Geneious/SeqBuilder, keep predecessor/family proxies visibly labelled. Rank descending by recent citations to that selected paper. The owner approved this representative-paper policy and the labelled proxies with the final cohort. Ties share rank. This is a proxy, not a count of software users: citing a tool does not establish current use, respect, correctness, or use of a particular version. Family/version proxies are labelled; the results remain descriptive evidence rather than votes.

Recent window is the three calendar publication years **2024–2026**, with2026 incomplete; it is not a rolling36-month interval. All counts were fetched from Europe PMC REST on2026-10-23. Total=record `citedByCount`; recent=`hitCount` for `CITES:PMID_MED AND PUB_YEAR:[2024 TO 2026]`. Links below give each exact query. No counts across papers were summed. Selecting a different version paper can change rankings: MEGA12=1462/1445, MEGA7=27508/4741; WashU2022=156/94 versus original273/23. These sensitivities are material.
